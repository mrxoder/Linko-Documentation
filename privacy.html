Privacy Policy

Linko — Privacy-first, self-hosted URL shortener

Effective date: January 2026

Introduction

Linko is a self-hosted URL shortening application. When you use Linko, all data is stored on your own server. We, as the developers, do not access, store, or share any data from your installation.

Data Collection

Linko does not collect or transmit any personal data to our servers. All analytics, including click counts, IP addresses, user-agents, countries, devices, and operating systems, are stored only locally on your server.

Third-Party Services

If you configure external services (e.g., SMTP for password recovery), your data may be transmitted to those services according to their respective privacy policies. We are not responsible for data handled by third-party services.

Cookies and Tracking

Linko does not use cookies or any third-party tracking tools by default. All tracking occurs locally on your server and under your control.

Your Responsibility

As the administrator, you are responsible for securing your server, managing users, and complying with local data privacy regulations. Linko provides the tools to manage and secure your data, but ultimate responsibility lies with you.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy periodically to reflect changes in software functionality or legal requirements. We recommend reviewing this page occasionally to stay informed about how your data is handled.
